Pingly

AI done. You know.

Pingly sits in your system tray and watches your AI coding agents. When one finishes or needs approval, a small card appears at the top of your screen — so a 20-second question never costs you 40 minutes.

  1. 1Hit Connect on each agent you actually use.
  2. 2Restart that agent so it loads the new hooks.
  3. 3Carry on working. Pingly stays hidden until something needs you.
